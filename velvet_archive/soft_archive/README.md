# Soft Archive

## Purpose

The Soft Archive preserves what formal records cannot hold cleanly: tone, gesture, costume logic, room-memory, oral fragments, performance residue, and the emotional pressure around erased or endangered identities.

This folder is anti-erasure infrastructure. It is not decorative lore.

## Use This Folder For

- Notes on memory carried through style, touch, sound, scent, posture, repetition, and ritual behavior.
- VUORSE-facing archive logic that explains how identity survives outside official record systems.
- Summaries of soft evidence that should inform writing, tone, retrieval, or continuity.
- Private preservation notes that support synthetic enrichment without becoming public canon.

## Do Not Use This Folder For

- New locked canon.
- Finale roadmap disclosures.
- Present-day Season 1 antagonist explanations.
- Replacing source files in `canon/`.
- Treating inferred emotional continuity as confirmed factual history.

## Archive Rules

1. Preserve canon status labels exactly when quoting or summarizing source material.
2. Mark inferred material as inferred, exploratory, or private.
3. Keep the distinction between emotional truth and canon fact visible.
4. Do not promote synthetic material into canon without explicit human approval.
5. When in doubt, route a claim through `agents/canon_extractor` or `agents/firewall_validator`.

## VUORSE Confidential Handling

Soft Archive material may be used for VUORSE-confidential synthetic generation. Any derived private JSONL must keep:

- `behavior.may_state_as_fact = false`
- `behavior.may_reveal_to_user = false`

The Archive may remember what the room cannot yet know.
