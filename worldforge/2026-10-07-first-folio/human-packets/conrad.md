# Sealed packet — Conrad
- **Basis:** `mixed` — dossier from both rosters; the seventy-year reading is an assumption on top of the canon-note.
- **Cutoff:** Late twelfth century, inside the wardrobe, before any name “Thumper” is written down, before Margarethe’s birth in 1271.
- **Knows:** The name Conrad is a nom de plume. The wardrobe is a disguise of about five hundred pounds, mechanized because a man cannot stroll about in it. Sunlight is the excuse he gives. Amber-scented coin stills a village faster than an argument. Stitches can carry what speech should not. Grey hair, no new wrinkles. Same man.
- **Does not know:** Federstahl, Vorst, Zerethium, the Professor, that a later folio will call the wardrobe mechanized, that cosmology will argue the cavern is a future wound. If he is Hildebrand, he knows the dark of the cavern from the inside. He does not know it was mailed backward from a laboratory that has not been built.
## Decision graph
- **A —** Keep the merchant’s name. Let the wardrobe be the whole of the public body. Pay for the monster rumors. Bury the seam-code in imperial lace.
- **B —** Step back into the cavern and finish the vanishing, so the next vessel can be born rather than continued.
- **C —** Open the wardrobe in daylight and teach the stitch as doctrine.
## Adjudication
Primary action for this run: **A**. `simulation` — steward ruling: direct recurrence. No diversion. He switches lanes. False only as a second person. Nom de plume. Five-hundred-pound disguise.
The locked doctrine is that the house chose family because history would stomach inheritance and would not stomach return. A half-ton box and a new name are that doctrine with wheels. Opening the door (C) spends the cover. A clean death (B) would have been a diversion, an elsewhere, and then a later return. He does not take it. He never leaves the road. Later scribes who say he recurred are right if they keep the word *direct*. They are wrong the moment they send him into the cavern and bring him out as someone else.
Rejected for the main chain: B, C. B remains alive as a branch if the steward wants Margarethe’s epitaph to be literally the first death.
