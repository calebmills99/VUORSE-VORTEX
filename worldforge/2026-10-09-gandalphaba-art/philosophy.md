# Sealcraft

Sealcraft is a generative aesthetic movement for governed glamour: a visual system where every luminous mark must earn its place through provenance, rank, relationship, and restraint. Its beauty is not decorative abundance, but the sensation of a schema enforcing elegance under pressure. The work should feel like a ritual interface built by someone who understands that a claim, a source, and a seal are different species of truth: the Steward seals, while Gandie implements the visible machinery around that boundary. It demands a meticulously crafted algorithm, refined through countless hours until the composition reads as inevitable rather than arranged.

Computationally, Sealcraft treats the canvas as a staged archive, never a silent mutation. Nodes emerge as provisional claims, statement ranks, and source anchors; edges pull them into provenance chains before any sparkle is allowed to matter. Conflicts are held in visible tension rather than smoothed away, because contradiction is a record awaiting a ruling, not a blemish to blur. The final algorithm must show master-level implementation: every curve, collision, density pocket, and empty interval should feel tuned by deep computational expertise, as if the artist spent days refining the difference between a noisy diagram and a ritual machine that happens to validate.

Randomness in Sealcraft is seeded, supervised, and answerable. Noise fields introduce glamour turbulence, but they never become mush; stochastic variation should behave like theatrical smoke around hard structural decisions. Validator green is treated as conditional fire, not truth: it appears only after the structure has demonstrated source coherence, and even then it glows as evidence of compilation rather than canon. This requires painstaking optimization and careful calibration, because glamour must compile and the controlled chaos must remain legible at a glance and rewarding under close inspection.

Color functions as status, not paint. Smoke gray and black-violet carry the field; parchment gold marks source gravity; silver traces the Gray Eye; acid green appears only as validator fire after the provenance logic holds. The algorithm should avoid blunt iconography. It should quote the drag-witch schema implementer through behavior: arched loops, severe triangles, citation charms, staged revisions, source-before-sparkle hierarchy, and a central watching aperture that is never a face. The result should be the product of deep computational expertise, not a costume rendered in code.

At its highest form, Sealcraft becomes a living proof that governance can be theatrical without becoming sloppy. Parameters expose the forces of the movement: node count, seal strength, provenance pull, temporal shear, glamour turbulence, and acid accent intensity. Each seed generates a different ritual plate from the same underlying discipline, where aesthetics serve governance and canon Markdown implies a matching source/JSON shadow rather than an isolated flourish. The implementation should feel obsessively refined, master-level, and meticulously crafted, with the elegance of a system that knows exactly what it is allowed to reveal.
