# Gandalphaba Sealcraft Production Design Bible

## Design Thesis

Gandalphaba's visual system is a theatrical schema workshop: camp glamour on the surface, hard operational rigor underneath. The viewer should understand in the first three seconds that the hero subject is not "a witch" but a governed drag-witch/wizard interface where the Gray Eye watches, provenance threads pull, source anchors hold, seals perform authority limits, and acid-green validation sparks mark trouble without crowning anything canon.

## World Premise

The visible world is an invented ritual workroom for Seam Schema 2.x. It is neither medieval fantasy tower nor generic cyber dashboard. It behaves like a staged archive: half dressing room, half evidence board, half validation console, which is mathematically rude because Gandalphaba would enjoy that. The world is theatrical, precise, and provisional. Every visible mark should imply implementation, audit, source handling, or report generation, never independent canon sealing.

## Tenets -> Visual Rules

- **Steward seals; Gandie implements.** Seals appear as restrained props around the system, never as a central crown on Gandalphaba. The Gray Eye observes and implements; it does not grant canon.
- **Source before sparkle.** Parchment-gold source anchors must be legible before acid-green glamour appears.
- **No canon without provenance.** Claims should visually connect to source anchors through gold rigging/thread before receiving emphasis.
- **Staged changes over silent mutation.** Temporal seams, arcs, and offset bands should show revision layers rather than pretending the graph has always been settled.
- **Schema is a ritual machine, not a filing cabinet.** Use circular mechanics, rigging, seal instruments, and operating geometry instead of static folder/file metaphors.
- **Validator green is not truth unless coverage is real.** Acid green appears only when a node has structural support and passes the Coverage Gate; unsupported conflict remains tense, not greenwashed.
- **Every canon Markdown addition must get JSON/source representation.** Pair prose-like glyphs with structured nodes, source anchors, or schema tablets.
- **Conflicts are preserved until ruled, not smoothed away.** Conflict nodes stay visibly suspended between competing pulls.
- **Aesthetics serve governance.** Beauty clarifies hierarchy, provenance, and authority limits; it never hides missing evidence.
- **Glamour must compile.** The composition may be camp, ornate, and theatrical, but it must remain readable, parameterized, and reproducible by seed.

## Set / Location Metaphor

The "set" is a schema stage seen from above. The central Gray Eye is the hero read and aperture. Around it, architectural arcs operate like stage curtains, index shelves, and temporal seams. Provenance chains behave as rigging thread connecting claims to sources. Seal rings are hero props placed like ritual instruments on the floor plan. Negative space matters: the sigil needs breathing room so the viewer reads hierarchy before detail.

## Hero Props

- **Gray Eye aperture**: central silver/smoke eye motif; the fastest read and primary continuity anchor.
- **Seal rings**: parchment-gold and acid-accented authority props; must feel deliberate, not decorative circles.
- **Schema/source tool language**: abstract `src`, `st`, `rel`, `act`, `rank`, `seal`, and `prov` marks around the field; use as marginalia, not busy labels.
- **Provenance rigging**: gold thread-like curves connecting nodes; should imply evidence chain and source dependency.

## Action / Evidence / Texture Props

- **Action props**: moving or tensioned provenance lines, validation sparks, rings that appear to clasp a claim.
- **Evidence props**: parchment-gold source anchors, statement-rank nodes, relationship triangles, activity tablets.
- **Texture props**: dust grain, paper flecks, smoky turbulence, faint seam bands, worn archive-floor rings.
- **Forbidden prop behavior**: no broomsticks, cauldrons, school-witch props, generic spell particles, fake awards, fake canon stamps, or literal legal seals.

## Color / Value / Material System

- **Base palette**: smoke gray and black-violet, matte and low-chroma, carrying most of the field.
- **Light/value plan**: bright parchment ground with a dark central hero read; mid-value ring structures; fine lines kept legible.
- **Metallic system**: silver belongs to the Gray Eye and precision lines; parchment gold belongs to sources, provenance, and seal hardware.
- **Accent system**: acid green is validator fire only. Use sparingly for failures, active glamour, or controlled emphasis.
- **Material logic**: parchment, smoked glass, tarnished silver, black-violet velvet, gold leaf, lacquer, ink, and fine thread. Avoid chrome sci-fi, neon cyberpunk, plastic UI glow, rainbow gradients, or flat mascot colors.

## Graphic / Environmental Design Rules

- Use glyphs as environmental graphics: abstract system labels are allowed, but exact claims should remain minimal or reserved for future compositing.
- Keep generated text short and symbolic; the artifact should not rely on readable paragraphs.
- Treat the canvas as a dashboard-world hybrid: interface controls live in the template sidebar; the art itself is a staged schema environment.
- Preserve the algorithmic-art viewer template branding and layout. Customize algorithmic content and parameter controls, not the overall UI container.
- Design for reuse across still headers, icons, dashboards, and ritual visuals by maintaining a strong central mark and scalable value structure.

## Continuity Anchors

- Adult drag-witch/wizard implication through theatrical rigor, sharp triangular forms, and glamorous severity, without literal character portraiture.
- Gray Eye motif always present and central or clearly dominant.
- Schema/source/provenance tools always present as lines, labels, anchors, or props.
- Smoke gray, black-violet, silver, parchment gold, and acid green remain the governing palette.
- Camp exterior plus operational rigor: witty, glamorous, exacting, never sloppy.
- Gandalphaba never appears to seal canon herself; visual language stays candidate/provisional unless the Steward rules otherwise.

## Forbidden Defaults

- Generic neon cyberpunk.
- Green-skinned witch, Wicked clone, broomstick schoolmarm, or Broadway-witch silhouette.
- Gandalf cosplay with makeup.
- Random particle soup with no hierarchy.
- Dense unreadable graph hairball.
- Fake canon stamps, official-looking legal seals, or authority language that implies promotion.
- Candy rainbow palette, marble-luxury cliché, gothic clutter without schema function.

## QA Checklist

- Can the viewer identify the Gray Eye/sigil hero subject in the first three seconds?
- Do provenance chains read as rigging/thread rather than random lines?
- Do source anchors feel parchment-gold and structurally important?
- Do seals read as hero props with authority limits, not decoration?
- Are validation failures or glamour accents acid green and sparse?
- Does validator green appear only after source coverage is structurally present?
- Does the composition avoid generic witch clip-art and generic cyberpunk?
- Does the palette maintain smoke gray, black-violet, silver, parchment gold, and acid green?
- Is the result usable as generated media direction, dashboard background, report header, skill icon exploration, and future summit ritual visual?
- Does the artifact remain provisional and avoid implying Gandalphaba seals canon herself?
