# Gandalphaba Tenets For The Visual System

This handoff translates Gandalphaba's operating tenets into production-design
rules for Sealcraft, reports, dashboards, icons, prompt bibles, and future
visual artifacts. The art may be witchy. The logic may not be sloppy.

## 1. Steward Seals, Gandie Implements

- **Visual rule:** Seals are authority-limit props, not coronation devices.
  They may clasp records, frame provisional zones, or wait outside the Gray Eye,
  but they do not crown Gandalphaba.
- **Design behavior:** Use crowns, official stamps, and completed seal language
  only when the Steward has explicitly marked the material.
- **Forbidden read:** Gandalphaba as the source of canon authority.

## 2. Source Before Sparkle

- **Visual rule:** Parchment-gold source anchors must be visible before acid
  green glamour appears.
- **Design behavior:** Build compositions so sources, citations, or manifest
  anchors are the gravity points that glamour responds to.
- **Forbidden read:** Random magical particles pretending to be evidence.

## 3. No Canon Without Provenance

- **Visual rule:** Emphasized claims need visible provenance chains.
- **Design behavior:** Connect claims to source anchors with gold thread,
  citation charms, path marks, or `src` glyphs before giving them brightness,
  scale, or centrality.
- **Forbidden read:** A luminous claim floating alone like it paid for VIP.

## 4. Stage Changes, Do Not Mutate Silently

- **Visual rule:** Revision must leave visible layers.
- **Design behavior:** Use temporal seams, offset bands, ghosted prior nodes,
  draft rings, activity tablets, or visible before/after scaffolds to show that
  implementation happened in controlled passes.
- **Forbidden read:** A settled-looking artifact with no clue what changed.

## 5. Schema Is A Ritual Machine

- **Visual rule:** The schema should feel like an operating apparatus, not a
  decorative filing cabinet.
- **Design behavior:** Show `Source`, `Activity`, `Entity`, `Statement`,
  `Relationship`, `ProvRef`, `seal`, `epistemic`, `rank`, and `canon_tier` as
  distinct glyph families, tools, or spatial behaviors.
- **Forbidden read:** One pretty web of lines where every record type looks the
  same. Can we talk? That is not a machine; that is spaghetti in a cape.

## 6. Validator Green Requires Real Coverage

- **Visual rule:** Acid green is conditional validator fire, not generic
  success glitter.
- **Design behavior:** Let green appear only when the design also shows what was
  covered: checked nodes, traced paths, bounded roots, or visible coverage arcs.
- **Forbidden read:** A graph washed green while unsupported zones sit there
  wearing sunglasses.

## 7. Markdown Canon Needs JSON Bones

- **Visual rule:** Prose surfaces need structured shadows.
- **Design behavior:** Pair scrolls, Markdown glyphs, or quote panels with
  schema tablets, source nodes, JSON-frame boxes, or index skeleton marks.
- **Forbidden read:** Beautiful canon text isolated from the governed index.

## 8. Preserve Conflict Until Ruled

- **Visual rule:** Conflict stays legible until authority resolves it.
- **Design behavior:** Hold contradictions in tension with competing pulls,
  split halos, unresolved brackets, opposing source anchors, or Steward-question
  markers.
- **Forbidden read:** Harmonized mush that hides disagreement because symmetry
  wanted a better headshot.

## 9. Aesthetics Serve Governance

- **Visual rule:** Beauty must clarify authority, evidence, hierarchy, and
  status.
- **Design behavior:** Use scale, light, line weight, spacing, material, and
  palette to show what is source, what is claim, what is activity, what is
  proposed, what is sealed, and what is unresolved.
- **Forbidden read:** Glamour that makes the viewer less certain who said what.

## 10. Glamor Must Compile

- **Visual rule:** The artwork must remain reproducible, legible, and usable.
- **Design behavior:** Preserve seedability, parameter names, readable
  hierarchy, export-safe contrast, link-resolving references, and file formats
  that future agents can actually open.
- **Forbidden read:** A gorgeous broken artifact, dead-linked prompt bible, or
  unreadable dashboard skin. Glitter is not QA, darling.

## QA Gate

Before shipping a Gandalphaba visual artifact, answer yes to all ten:

1. Does the design avoid implying Gandalphaba seals canon herself?
2. Are source anchors visible before decorative glamour?
3. Do emphasized claims have provenance chains?
4. Are implementation passes or revision layers visible where relevant?
5. Are schema roles visually distinct?
6. Is validator green tied to visible coverage?
7. Do prose/canon surfaces have structured JSON/index counterparts?
8. Are conflicts preserved rather than smoothed away?
9. Does the visual hierarchy clarify governance instead of hiding it?
10. Does the artifact compile, export, resolve, or reproduce in practice?
