# Season 1 Private Roadmap

## Purpose

This folder holds private Season 1 development scaffolding that is not automatically safe for the writers room.

The public-facing Season 1 spine remains: Jake McCullen learning that the wound beneath his ranch and the silence around his mother are the same wound.

## Use This Folder For

- Private Season 1 structure questions.
- Reveal-order planning that should not appear in room-facing docs yet.
- Episode, object, symbol, or relationship constraints that require user approval.
- Notes about what the room should feel without being told.
- VUORSE-confidential scaffolding for future synthesis or continuity checks.

## Do Not Use This Folder For

- Replacing `docs/writers-room/season-1-spine.md`.
- Spoiling finale material.
- Defining a present-day external antagonist for the Wyoming story.
- Revealing Jake's mother's unresolved name.
- Promoting private roadmap theory into canon.

## Season 1 Boundary

For room-facing work:

- Wyoming is the entry point.
- Jake's refusal supplies pseudo-antagonistic pressure.
- The season must feel like something is missing.
- VUORSE may frame the mystery without solving it too early.
- The mythology serves the wound, not the reverse.

Private roadmap material may guide restraint. It must not leak into the room as explanation.

## Review Checklist

Before promoting anything from this folder:

1. Identify whether it belongs in private roadmap, writers room, or canon.
2. Check for finale leakage.
3. Check for antagonist leakage.
4. Check for mother-name leakage.
5. Route continuity concerns through canon review.
