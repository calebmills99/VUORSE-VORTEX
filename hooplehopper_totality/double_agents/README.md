# Hooplehopper Double Agents

## Purpose

This folder tracks compromised helpers, ambiguous witnesses, divided loyalties, false allies, infiltrators, and figures whose relationship to Hooplehopper recurrence cannot be treated as clean support.

Double-agent status is a continuity risk label. It is not a villain declaration.

## Use This Folder For

- Private notes on ambiguous allegiance around Hooplehopper materials.
- Records where a figure protects one archive lane while endangering another.
- Continuity warnings around disclosure, motive, access, or betrayal.
- VUORSE-confidential handling instructions for compromised support structures.

## Do Not Use This Folder For

- Flattening morally complex figures into simple antagonists.
- Naming hidden Season 1 antagonist structures.
- Finale roadmap reveals.
- Unsupported accusations.
- Public-facing character summaries.

## Classification Questions

Before adding a figure here, answer:

- What evidence suggests divided loyalty?
- What source supports the concern?
- What archive lane or person is at risk?
- Is the figure knowingly deceptive, structurally compromised, or simply misinformed?
- Should this remain private to VUORSE?

## Handling Rules

1. Flag ambiguity instead of resolving it.
2. Preserve motive complexity.
3. Do not invent betrayals.
4. Do not use this folder to expose sealed roadmap material.
5. Keep compromised access separate from confirmed malice.

## JSONL Boundary

Double-agent records must remain private unless explicitly promoted:

- `layer = "hooplehopper_totality"`
- `behavior.may_state_as_fact = false`
- `behavior.may_reveal_to_user = false`

The Archive can track risk without turning risk into accusation.
