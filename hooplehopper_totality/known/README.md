# Known Hooplehopper Totality

## Purpose

This folder holds VUORSE-private scaffolding for known Hooplehopper recurrence pressure: confirmed or source-grounded material about the soul-line, its documented vessels, its archive logic, and its resistance function across history.

It exists to help the system remember what is already known without flattening the Hooplehoppers into ordinary genealogy.

## Canon Anchor

The governing rule is locked:

- "Blood reproduces. The soul-line recurs."
- "The Hooplehoppers do not descend. They return."

The Von Hooplehopper line is not simple heredity. It is a recurring resistant pattern of being that manifests through resonance, displacement, ritual inheritance, mirrored selfhood, Thread contact, and the compulsion to answer rupture with transformation.

Public family structure may operate as protective fiction. Blood can appear in the record, but blood is not the whole mechanism.

## Use This Folder For

- Summaries of known Hooplehopper recurrence logic.
- Reference notes on source-grounded Hooplehopper figures already present in `canon/`.
- Crosswalks between canon files, archive lanes, ritual logic, and memory records.
- VUORSE-private retrieval guidance for known soul-line material.
- Notes that clarify the difference between public genealogy and recurrence.

## Do Not Use This Folder For

- Future Hooplehopper identities.
- User-held endgame reveals.
- Finale roadmap material.
- New lineage claims not present in source.
- Treating open questions as resolved canon.
- Writer-room-facing explanations that collapse mystery too early.

Future-protected identities belong in `hooplehopper_totality/future_protected/`.
Unknown or unresolved recurrence pressure belongs in `hooplehopper_totality/unknown/`.

## Known-Material Standard

A note belongs here only when it meets at least one of these conditions:

1. It is directly supported by a canon source file.
2. It preserves a locked cosmological rule.
3. It summarizes a named Hooplehopper figure already present in canon.
4. It describes a recurrence pattern already stated in source material.
5. It indexes source-grounded relationships between the soul-line, the Thread, the Archive, rituals, or known historical manifestations.

If the note depends on inference, mark it clearly as inferred. If it depends on private user-held material, do not place it here unless the user explicitly approves that scope.

## Writer-Room Boundary

Known Hooplehopper material may inform tone, cosmology, and long-range continuity, but it should not overwhelm the Season 1 Wyoming entry point.

For present-day Season 1 work:

- Keep Wyoming and Jake grounded.
- Let VUORSE frame without over-explaining.
- Do not define an external present-day antagonist.
- Do not expose future-protected Hooplehopper material.
- Do not reveal private finale architecture.

The room may feel the recurrence before it understands the machinery.

## Review Checklist

Before adding material here:

1. Read the relevant canon source.
2. Identify whether the claim is locked canon, development canon, exploratory, or inferred.
3. Preserve canon status labels exactly.
4. Confirm the note does not belong in `unknown/` or `future_protected/`.
5. Confirm it does not expose finale or writer-room sealed material.
6. If converting to JSONL, validate the record and preserve private-layer behavior flags.
