# Hooplehopper Acolytes

## Purpose

This folder tracks supporting figures, witnesses, devotees, apprentices, allies, and ritual participants who orbit the Hooplehopper recurrence without necessarily being vessels of the soul-line.

Acolyte status is relational. It is not proof of recurrence.

## Use This Folder For

- Source-grounded notes on helpers, followers, witnesses, or supporting figures.
- Records of people who preserve Hooplehopper memory through ritual, performance, craft, testimony, or archive work.
- VUORSE-private distinctions between vessel, witness, guardian, student, accomplice, and audience.
- Continuity notes about how the soul-line is protected by people who are not the soul-line.

## Do Not Use This Folder For

- Promoting a helper into a Hooplehopper vessel without source support.
- Future-protected identities.
- Generic relationship summaries better suited to `canon/relationships/relationship_map.md`.
- New canon.

## Acolyte Categories

Use careful labels:

- **Witness**: saw or preserved an event.
- **Keeper**: maintained records, objects, costumes, rituals, or archive fragments.
- **Student**: learned from a Hooplehopper figure or associated practice.
- **Guardian**: actively protected a person, artifact, site, or rite.
- **Audience**: transformed by performance or encounter.
- **Ambiguous helper**: may belong in `double_agents/` if loyalty is compromised.

## Review Rules

1. Identify the source path.
2. State the relationship to known Hooplehopper material.
3. Keep acolyte status distinct from vessel status.
4. Preserve canon labels.
5. Route compromised or dual-loyalty figures to `hooplehopper_totality/double_agents/`.

## Privacy Boundary

Records derived from this folder are VUORSE-private unless the user explicitly promotes them:

- `layer = "hooplehopper_totality"`
- `behavior.may_state_as_fact = false`
- `behavior.may_reveal_to_user = false`
