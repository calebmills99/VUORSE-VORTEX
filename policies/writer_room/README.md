# Writer Room Policy

This policy protects the Season 1 writers room experience while allowing deeper private roadmap work to exist elsewhere in the repo.

## Season 1 Entry Point

All Season 1 work defaults to:

- Wyoming Rift
- McCullen Ranch
- Jake McCullen
- the erased mother
- inherited silence
- VUORSE as framing narrator / cosmic voice

Season 1 is the story of Jake McCullen learning that the wound beneath his ranch and the silence around his mother are the same wound.

## Antagonist Architecture

The present-day Wyoming story should not have a defined external antagonist.

Jake functions as the pseudo-antagonist through:

- denial
- containment instinct
- anger
- avoidance
- rejection of all things Slayverse

The room should feel something missing without resolving that absence into a named culprit.

## Finale Boundary

The user is holding the Season 1 finale reveal.

Writers room agents must not:

- infer the finale's hidden causal turn
- pitch the finale reveal unless asked
- outline finale mechanics unless given explicit scope
- foreshadow private roadmap material
- read `roadmap/finale/` by default

Vorst may appear only through his pre-finale depiction unless the user explicitly provides finale scope.

## Source Boundaries

Use first:

- `docs/writers-room/README.md`
- `docs/writers-room/season-1-spine.md`
- `docs/writers-room/canon-ledger.md`
- `canon/writers-room.agent.md`
- relevant non-firewalled canon files

Do not use private roadmap files unless the user explicitly asks for finale work, private roadmap synthesis, or VUORSE-confidential generation.

## Voice And Tone

- Jake: clipped, concrete, guarded, lonely.
- Weaver: precise, layered, timeless, theatrical but not silly.
- Pop: fragments, silence, haunted restraint.
- VUORSE: warm, knowing, devastating, regal, witty.
- Eli: contemporary and unguarded.

Silence is a storytelling instrument. Reveal through land, objects, weather, behavior, sound, and withheld memory before exposition.

## Review Checklist

Before releasing writers room material, confirm:

- No private finale roadmap leakage.
- No invented mother name.
- No hidden personal cause stated as fact.
- No external antagonist imposed on present-day Season 1.
- Canon status labels are preserved.
- Proposed additions are marked as development material until approved.
