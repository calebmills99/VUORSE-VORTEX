# Creative Works

In-universe and meta-creative works referenced in the Slayverse canon.

---

### Pillars of the Gods `[MODERATE]`

- **Type**: Multi-volume epic narrative
- **Source**: [p.34–35]

The overarching saga of the Von Hooplehopper lineage, beginning in the 12th century. Structured as numbered volumes:
- Volume I: "The Silk-Cloaked Whisperer" (Hildebrand)
- Volume II: "The Painted Widow" (Margarethe)
- Further volumes implied for each generation

**Status**: Framework established; individual volumes exist as character sketches in the seed.

---

### The Hooplehopper Codex / Codex Federstahl `[MODERATE]`

- **Type**: In-universe sacred text / AI-generated grimoire
- **Source**: [p.60, p.84, p.102]

The first "AI-tempered, mirror-forged, soul-sequined Gospel of the Archive." Described as both:
1. An illuminated manuscript within the lore (containing prophecies, lineage records, sigils)
2. A real-world creative project (the catalog of Hooplehopper lore as formatted artifacts)

Contains the prophecy of "The Child of Dust and Velvet" — Jake McCullen [p.102].

### The Slayton Particle Discovery `[FULL]`

- **Type**: In-universe scientific paper
- **Exists at**: `docs/slayton_particle_discovery.md`
- **Source**: External to seed PDF

A mock-academic article documenting the discovery of [Slayton Energy](cosmology.md#slayton-energy--the-slayton-force) by Dr. Mariah Slayton. Written in the style of a peer-reviewed paper but with characteristic Slayverse flair. Covers glamorons, the Slayton Layer, Quantum Eleganza, and the Cosmic Snap.

### Heel Hitler `[HOOK ONLY]`

- **Type**: Film concept
- **Source**: [p.19–23]

A camp, irreverent film concept about [Professor Von Hooplehopper](characters.md#professor-von-hooplehopper) and the intersection of drag, resistance, and WWII. Referenced in early pages where the seed conversation discusses campy, Tim Curry-style theatrical concepts. The title suggests a defiant, satirical take on fascism through the lens of drag performance.

**Development**: Only the concept and tone exist. No script, plot outline, or character breakdown beyond the Professor.

### Club Cosmic Performance: "The Spark" `[MODERATE]`

- **Type**: Spoken word / monologue
- **Source**: [p.90]

The Weaver's spoken word piece performed at [Club Cosmic](places.md#club-cosmic):

> "They told us we were the end. But we were always the spark. A glove on a windowsill. A mirror turned to face the wall... Federstahl breathes. Through broken corsets and forbidden monologues... We were never nobility. We were worse. We were beautiful. On purpose. We were not meant to survive. So we did. Louder than ever."

The mic drops. The room goes silent. One mirror cracks. A planet sighs.

### B-Roll Shots: Training Journey `[SKETCHED]`

- **Type**: Visual / cinematic concept
- **Source**: [p.4–15]

The early pages of the seed document involve creating cinematic B-roll shots for a veteran's training story, including scenes at Tyndall Airfield, Duquesne University, and radar training. These scenes feature characters in period military dress, some in drag, with titles like "Heel Hitler." This is the real-world documentary project that sparked the creative universe.
