## Television Season Arcs

### Season 1 — The Wound Under the Prairie `[LOCKED]`

- **Type**: TV season arc
- **Primary Location**: [McCullen Ranch / Powder River Basin](places.md#mcullen-ranch--powder-river-basin)
- **Primary Characters**: [Jake McCullen](characters/characters.md#jake-mccullen), [The Weaver](characters/characters.md#the-weaver), [Pop](characters/characters.md#pop), [Eli McCullen](characters/characters.md#eli-mccullen), [VUORSE](characters/characters.md#miss-slaytonia-vuorse)
- **Source**: [`canon/SLAYVERSE_TV_SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `docs/writers-room/season-1-spine.md`]

Wyoming as local mystery, temporal horror, and inherited guardianship. The story never leaves the ranch for long. The horror is domestic: a family that broke time and then refused to talk about it.

**Starting Thesis**: Season 1 is the story of Jake McCullen learning that the wound beneath his ranch and the silence around his mother are the same wound.

**Emotional Spine**:
1. **Denial** — Jake treats the Weaver as a trespasser, the fissure as geological, and the family history as none of anyone's business.
2. **Confrontation** — Evidence accumulates that cannot be explained away; the brand on Jake's yearling is revealed as a Federstahl cipher.
3. **Revelation** — Pop's secret unfolds: the singing, the buried crystal, the failed ribbon gates, and the graves.
4. **Grief Spoken Aloud** — Jake speaks his mother's name, and the fissure responds.

**Canon Notes**:
- Every Season 1 story choice should move Jake closer to speaking his mother's name aloud.
- The mythology serves the wound, not the reverse.
- The present-day Wyoming story should not be structured around a defined external antagonist.
- Jake functions as the pseudo-antagonist through his denial, containment instinct, anger, and rejection of all things Slayverse.
- The user is holding the finale reveal; the room should not infer it from Season 1 materials.
- Save the prairie mother, save the universe.

## Mythic And Cosmological Arcs

### The Federstahl Lattice Incident `[FULL]`

- **Type**: Cosmological catastrophe / ontological deletion event
- **Source**: [`canon/cosmology/cosmology.md`; `canon/organizations/organizations.md`; `canon/SLAYVERSE_TV_SERIES_BIBLE.md`]

The Federstahl Institute attempted to trap time in solid form using lattice-bound tachyon particles. They succeeded, and the crystal screamed. The resulting incident erased people from existence and scattered them across time, creating seven temporal fissures.

**Canon Notes**:
- The Federstahl Lattice Incident is an ontological deletion event, not merely a time fracture. `[LOCKED]`
- The catastrophe dispersed erased persons across history as temporal anomalies. `[LOCKED]`
- The Wyoming Rift is the last active and unclaimed fissure.

### The Jake / Weaver Confrontation `[FULL]`

- **Type**: Season 1 dramatic engine
- **Source**: [`canon/SLAYVERSE_TV_SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `canon/rituals/rituals.md`; `canon/relationships/relationship_map.md`]

The Weaver arrives in Wyoming knowing about the fissure, Pop, and Jake's unnamed mother. The confrontation forces Jake toward the truth he has refused: the land is not haunted; it is wounded, and the wound was made by his own ancestors.

**Key Function**:
- Converts cosmology into personal grief.
- Pushes Jake toward the final act of speaking his mother's name aloud.
- Keeps Season 1 anchored in Wyoming and the McCullen Ranch.
