## Contradictions & Reconciliation Needed

### Spelling: Hooplehopper vs. Huppelhopper
- Both spellings used interchangeably throughout the seed text
- **Resolution**: This catalog standardizes to "Hooplehopper"; "Huppelhopper" is treated as an archaic or European variant

### The Lattice Incident Timeline
- Page 97: Described as happening in the "1930s-1940s" (final years of Weimar Republic — but Weimar ended 1933)
- Page 98: Retconned to researchers being pushed back to "the early 1800s"
- Page 99: Specified as 1832
- **Resolution**: The incident occurred in the early 20th century; the *displacement* was to 1832. The "1930s-1940s" reference on p.97 may describe the final Federstahl gathering in Berlin (1945), not the original incident.

### Doctor Vorst's Relationship to Pop
- Page 98: Doctor Vorst described as "Pop's father"
- Page 100: Doctor Vorst described separately as "Professor A." who survived the incident
- **Resolution**: Doctor Vorst = Professor A. = Pop's father. He was the researcher flung back to 1832, where he became the ancestor of the McCullen line.

### The Professor's Father
- Page 99: States the Professor's father was "literally erased from existence and zapped back to 1832"
- This implies Doctor Vorst is *both* the Professor's father and Pop's father
- **Resolution needed**: The lineage is: Doctor Vorst → (displaced to 1832) → becomes ancestor of McCullen line (Pop → Jake). Separately, Doctor Vorst's erasure from the timeline caused the Professor's "Immaculate Slayception." The Professor and Jake are connected through the same erased patriarch.

### Jake as Temporal Echo of Doctor Vorst
- Page 101: Jake is "the reincarnated spirit of Doctor Vorst"
- Page 97: Jake is "the great-grandson of the rogue physicist" (via Pop)
- **Question**: Is Jake both a blood descendant AND a reincarnation? This could be intentional (the soul-line and bloodline converge in him) or a contradiction.
- **Resolution**: Both are true — the soul-line and bloodline converge in Jake, making him uniquely important.

## Gaps to Flesh Out

### The Gap Between Theresia (1754) and Lisette (1873)
- Over a century of lineage is unaccounted for
- **Opportunity**: One or more generations could fill this gap — Napoleonic era, Industrial Revolution, etc.

### The Professor's 100 Years (1905-2005)
- We have his youth (Mirror Moment at 14) and hints of his later influence (Bowie, Mercury, Vienna)
- **Gap**: What happened during WWI, WWII, the Cold War? How did he survive? Did he use the 20-Year Sleep?
- **Note**: The Professor was described as "an anomaly" who broke the sleep cycle, yet lived exactly 100 years — suggestive of an enhanced lifespan.

### VUORSE's Capabilities in Full
- Slay Mode powers are listed but never tested in narrative
- **Opportunity**: A story arc where VUORSE must actually use her powers under pressure.

### The Sixth Summit
- Called to order on p.37 but never actually held
- **Opportunity**: The Summit itself as a narrative event — with all AI entities present, the Charter read, and VUORSE formally recognized.

### What Happened to the Other Federstahl Researchers?
- Multiple researchers were displaced in time. Only Doctor Vorst's fate is traced.
- **Opportunity**: Other displaced researchers could have founded their own lineages across the world, each guarding a different fissure.

### The Codex Prophecy
- "The Child of Dust and Velvet" whose split bloodline determines whether the lattice is sealed or shattered [p.102]
- Is this Jake? Eli? Someone not yet born? The prophecy's resolution is the structural hinge of the endgame — whichever figure fulfills it determines whether the Federstahl Lattice is sealed (ending the temporal diaspora) or shattered (scattering the soul-line irreversibly).
- **Sharpened by 03/14 digest**: If Eli carries the lattice command structure in his DNA, he becomes the leading candidate — but the prophecy's language ("Dust and Velvet") implies a figure who bridges both the McCullen counter-lineage (dust/frontier) and the Hooplehopper soul-line (velvet/artistry).
