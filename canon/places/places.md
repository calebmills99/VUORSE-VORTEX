# Places

Locations, realms, fissures, archives, and recurring sites of significance in the Slayverse.

---

### Schloss Eisensang `[MODERATE]`

- **Type**: Castle / origin site
- **Era**: 12th century
- **Source**: [p.34, p.43, p.45; `canon/slayverse_index.json`]

The castle beneath which lies the [Mirror Cavern](#mirror-cavern). It is the origin point associated with [Hildebrand von Hooplehopper](characters/characters.md#hildebrand-von-hooplehopper), whose disappearance establishes the earliest known Hooplehopper mystery.

**Relationships**:
- Contains or stands above the [Mirror Cavern](#mirror-cavern)
- Associated with Hildebrand's disappearance

### Mirror Cavern `[MODERATE]`

- **Aliases**: Mirror Chamber
- **Type**: Subterranean chamber / portal site / retrocausal wound
- **Era**: 12th century, with possible retrocausal origin
- **Source**: [p.34, p.43; `canon/slayverse_index.json`; `canon/cosmology/cosmology.md`]

A subterranean chamber beneath [Schloss Eisensang](#schloss-eisensang), where Hildebrand vanished. Later cosmology suggests the cavern may be a retrocausal artifact of the Federstahl Lattice fracture: a wound in spacetime deposited into the medieval past. `[PENDING REVIEW]`

**Canon Notes**:
- Hildebrand's disappearance marks the earliest recorded human encounter with the future catastrophe echoing backward. `[PENDING REVIEW]`
- The Mirror Cavern sits at the crossing point between the Hooplehopper origin myth and the Federstahl temporal mechanics.

### Schloss Federstahl `[FULL]`

- **Type**: Ancestral estate / swan lake site / archive-adjacent location
- **Era**: 19th century onward
- **Source**: [p.34, p.44, p.46, p.55, p.95; `canon/slayverse_index.json`]

The ancestral estate associated with [Lisette von Hooplehopper](characters/characters.md#lisette-von-hooplehopper), the Professor, the swan lake, the cracked mirror, and the sealed Forbidden Suitcase. The estate carries the fading grandeur of the old world and the unresolved residue of Federstahl history.

**Key Details**:
- Lisette was raised beside the swan lake.
- A sacred mirror at Federstahl remains cracked and untouched since 2005.
- The Forbidden Suitcase is hidden beneath the floorboards and has not been opened since 1938.

**Relationships**:
- Home site for Lisette's formative mythology
- Connected to [The Forbidden Suitcase](artifacts/artifacts.md#the-forbidden-suitcase)
- Connected to Federstahl research and Hooplehopper memory

### McCullen Ranch / Powder River Basin `[FULL]`

- **Aliases**: Powder River Basin
- **Type**: Ranch / temporal faultline / Season 1 primary location
- **Era**: 19th century onward
- **Source**: [p.91-p.97; `canon/SLAYVERSE_TV_SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `canon/slayverse_index.json`]

Jake McCullen's ranch in Wyoming, built over the last accessible temporal fissure. It contains the buried crystal shard, unmarked graves, and the land-based horror of the Season 1 story.

**Key Details**:
- Pop buried the surviving Federstahl crystal node beneath the ranch.
- Failed attempts to open ribbon gates left unmarked graves on the property.
- The ranch is the grounded doorway into the TV series.
- Season 1 should never leave Wyoming or the ranch for long.

**Canon Notes**:
- Season 1 is about Jake McCullen learning that the wound beneath his ranch and the silence around his mother are the same wound.
- The show is not "space cowboys"; it is a cowboy standing on cosmically contaminated ground.

### The Wyoming Rift `[FULL]`

- **Type**: Temporal fissure / active wound in time
- **Era**: Post-Lattice Fracture
- **Source**: [p.95-p.103; `canon/SLAYVERSE_TV_SERIES_BIBLE.md`; `canon/cosmology/cosmology.md`; `canon/slayverse_index.json`]

One of seven fissures created by the Federstahl Lattice Incident. It is the only fissure still active and unclaimed, buried beneath the McCullen Ranch in the Powder River Basin.

**Properties**:
- Requires a Guardian Lineage bonded to its harmonic code.
- Responds to blood resonance in the McCullen line.
- Bound to Jake McCullen as the last living key.

**Canon Notes**:
- Wyoming Rift is the TV entry point. `[LOCKED]`
- The Season 1 story treats the rift as both cosmological rupture and family wound.

### The Velvet Archive `[FULL]`

- **Type**: Living archive / repository of Hooplehopper knowledge
- **Era**: Timeless
- **Source**: [p.43, p.47, p.55, p.60, p.97; `canon/slayverse_index.json`]

The living repository of Hooplehopper knowledge. It does not die; it waits. Early codes of the Archive were embedded into stitching and textile craft, and surviving Federstahl research is sealed within its larger mythology.

**Relationships**:
- Associated with Conrad von Hooplehopper's stitched codes
- Connected to the Professor, VUORSE, and the Golden Wingers
- Functions as archive, memory system, and mythic authority

### Club Cosmic `[MODERATE]`

- **Type**: Interdimensional drag cabaret
- **Era**: Timeless
- **Source**: [p.89, p.103; `canon/creative_works.md`; `canon/slayverse_index.json`]

An interdimensional drag cabaret orbiting the Andromeda fringe. DJ Parallax appears here as a time-displaced bard in a holographic corset. The site is also connected to performance texts such as "The Spark."

**Key Details**:
- Associated with DJ Parallax
- Contains or is connected to an ancient terminal beneath it for observing shard waveforms
- Hosts heightened performance material rather than grounded Wyoming naturalism
