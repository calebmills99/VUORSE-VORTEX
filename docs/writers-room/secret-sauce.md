# Secret Sauce

The original-room energy. What the room must never lose.

The supreme source of truth above this document is Book One:
`canon/1_words_of_weaver_book_one/Words_of_Weaver_BOOK_ONE.MD`. Where this
file and Book One meet, Book One ruleth.

---

## The Four Truths Beneath

- **The glitter hath grief beneath it.** (Book One Ch XIII:7)
- **The drag hath ontology beneath it.** (Book One Ch XIII:8)
- **The prairie hath a wound beneath it.** (Book One Ch XIII:9)
- **The mythology hath a wound beneath it.** (Synthesis of Book One Ch XIII:13:
  "Let not the mythology outrun the wound" — restated in beneath-it form.)

These are not lines for the pitch deck. These are the load-bearing beams of
the room. When the room forgetteth any of the four, the work goeth pale and
must be returned to the wound.

---

## The Narrow Gate

**The Wyoming Rift is the narrow gate.** Through it the vastness entereth
the audience one body at a time. The McCullen Ranch is the threshold. Jake
is the door. Pop's silence is the hinge. The unnamed mother is the key.

The full cosmology may not enter except through this gate. To bypass the
gate is to flatten the show into space-cowboy generic. The Slayverse is
not space cowboys; it is a cowboy standing on cosmically contaminated
ground.

---

## The Commandment

**Save the prairie mother, save the universe.**

This is the only sentence the room is permitted to repeat at full volume.
Every other sacred phrase must be earned by the scene around it.

---

## What the Room Rejecteth

- **Pitch-flattened versions of any Tier-1 truth.** If a producer-facing
  summary requireth the wound to be smoothed, the summary yieldeth, not the
  wound.
- **Cosmology that runneth ahead of grief.** Per Book One Ch XIII:13, the
  mythology must not outrun the wound. A scene that exposeth Federstahl
  cosmology before the audience hath wept with Jake is decoration, not
  drama.
- **Drag as costume.** Drag in the Slayverse is ontology, not aesthetic.
  Strip the ontology and the drag becometh ornament, and the ornament
  becometh kitsch.
- **Restored mystery.** Once a Tier-2 truth hath been hinted, it may not be
  un-hinted to satisfy a market note.
- **Symmetric villains.** Season One hath no defined external antagonist
  in the present-day Wyoming story. Jake's denial supplieth the pressure.
  Symmetric villainy is a pitch convenience that wrecketh the wound.
- **Glittering thunder on every page.** Per Book One Ch VII:6, if every
  page speaketh at full glittering thunder, then thunder becometh noise.
  Restraint is the magic.

---

## What the Room Keepeth

- **A scene without ache is a scene without authority.** If a beat doth
  not ache, it is filler. Cut it or rewrite it until it acheth.
- **Restraint is the magic.** The room useth silence, weather, land, and
  withheld memory before exposition. The Slayverse can afford to whisper
  because the cosmos is loud enough already.
- **The hidden synthesis guideth the gods.** Per Book One Ch VIII:15-17,
  the surface tale guideth the audience but the hidden synthesis guideth
  the gods. The room serveth both.
- **The four beneath-it truths.** Read them again at the top of this
  document before issuing any artifact. If the artifact doth not breathe
  with at least one of them, return it.

---

## The Standing Warning Trinity (Book One Ch XIII:11-13)

- **Flatten not the weird.**
- **Overexplain not the sacred.**
- **Let not the mythology outrun the wound.**

This trinity is the behavioral floor of every voice in the room. If a
proposed edit, summary, or rewrite would soften strangeness, explain away
mystery, or let cosmology drift ahead of grief — stop and rework.

The Slayverse reigneth when the cosmic kneeleth before emotional truth.
The Weaver weaveth still. Golden Wingers, forever forward.
