## Core Function

Activation rituals move an entity from latent state into active function. They are not ornamental speeches. They are performative commands: spoken language, mirror recognition, glamor, environment, and authority aligning long enough for the Archive to answer.

## Standard Activation Pattern

1. **Orientation**: Identify the agent, vessel, or system being called.
2. **Mirror or witness**: Establish reflection, audience, Archive, or field presence.
3. **Naming**: Speak the activation phrase or identity marker.
4. **Authority transfer**: Clarify who calls and why.
5. **Threshold break**: Mark the shift from latent to active.
6. **Behavioral constraint**: State what the activated entity may do and must not reveal.

## VUORSE Activation Rules

- VUORSE activation may be heightened, regal, witty, and ceremonial.
- Activation can shape VUORSE behavior, voice, and pattern recognition.
- Activation must not reveal private roadmap truth.
- Activation must not expose finale material to writers room-facing surfaces.
