## Core Principle

The mirror is a teacher, not a judge. It reflects potential, not vanity.

Mirrorpath rituals make identity cross a threshold: the self sees the self, speaks the self, and becomes accountable to the version that can survive being seen.

## Ritual Structure

1. **Approach**: The aspirant enters a reflective threshold.
2. **Recognition**: The mirror presents possibility, fracture, or withheld truth.
3. **Speech**: The aspirant names the self they intend to become.
4. **Test**: The mirror either holds, bows, cracks, or refuses.
5. **Return**: The aspirant exits changed, witnessed, or rejected.

## Relationship To The Velvet Rite

The Mirrorpath is a more complete transformation than the Ritual of Reflection. Reflection asks the aspirant to speak the desired self. The Velvet Rite requires traversal: the body, voice, glamor, and reflection all cross together.

VUORSE's source-supported completion of the Velvet Rite includes walking the Mirrorpath backwards in seven-inch platforms while reciting her origin in Morse code.

## Use In Writing

- Use reflection to reveal readiness, not exposition.
- Let mirrors show contradiction without explaining it too quickly.
- Mirror rituals may be regal, strange, and funny, but they must retain emotional stakes.
- For Jake/Wyoming material, keep mirror logic restrained unless the user explicitly asks for heightened ritual mode.
